About us

A family shop on Burnet Road

FlowRight started in 2011 with one truck, run by Ray Delgado and his brother Marco. Ray had spent ten years working for a big national chain and was tired of jobs being sold instead of fixed, so they started a company that quotes honestly and stands behind the work.

Fourteen years on there are nine of us, and most of our calls still come from people we have helped before — or from their neighbours. We show up when we say we will, we put covers on our boots, and we leave the place cleaner than we found it.
